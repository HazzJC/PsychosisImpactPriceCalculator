## [1.0.0] - 2026-09-19

### Added
- **NHS Relapse Cost Estimator**: Single-page economic model for NHS Mental Health Trust commissioners and finance teams.
- **Interactive Caseload Modeling**: Configurable service user population (100–5,000), relapse rates, and inpatient bed day durations.
- **Impact Derivations**: Calculations for annual relapses prevented, total financial cost savings, and bed days avoided under a 50% reduction assumption.
- **Live Tool**: Deployed on GitHub Pages at [hazzjc.github.io/PsychosisImpactPriceCalculator](https://hazzjc.github.io/PsychosisImpactPriceCalculator/).
